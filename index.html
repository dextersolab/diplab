HOW IT WORKS

four agents, one verdict

01

SCAN

pulls the top holders of any token straight off chain - no wallet connect, no signature.

02

TRACE

reads how each whale exited across past tokens to learn the multiple they habitually sell at.

03

MAP

projects those exits into walls, dips and one scenario line threading them on the chart.

04

SCORE

factors psychological levels and rates the token's exit-risk from 100 clean to 0 run.
